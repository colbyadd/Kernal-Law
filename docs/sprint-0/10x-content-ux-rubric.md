# 10/10 Content + UX Rubric

Updated: 2026-02-18
Owner: Todd Kernal (final approval), Codex implementation support

## Purpose

Create one scoring system for all money pages so decisions are objective and repeatable.

- `Content score`: 0-100
- `UX score`: 0-100
- `10/10 threshold`: 90+ on both axes, plus all non-negotiable gates passed.

## Non-Negotiable Gates (Must Pass)

1. Primary intent is clear in first viewport (service, market, and next action).
2. Page has a single primary CTA path and at least one low-friction alternate path (call/text/form).
3. No misleading legal claims, no guarantees, and no stale legal references.
4. No thin location swaps: each market page includes unique local legal context.
5. Mobile interaction quality is stable (menus, tap targets, forms, readable spacing).
6. Terms/Privacy/SMS consent language is consistent across form and policy pages.

## Content Rubric (100 points)

1. Intent Match and Search Fit (15)
- Does the page directly match the likely query intent?
- Is the opening section immediately aligned to that intent?

2. Legal Substance and Strategy Depth (20)
- Specific legal/process guidance, not generic copy.
- Practical decision points and risk framing.

3. Market Uniqueness and Local Authority (20)
- Venue/county/corridor specificity.
- Distinct local facts and enforcement/court context.

4. Proof and Credibility (15)
- Case outcomes, reviews, attorney authority, or process proof.
- Trust modules are visible before final CTA.

5. Information Architecture Inside Page (10)
- Logical section order and narrative flow.
- Clear section labeling for scan behavior.

6. Internal Linking and Topic Cluster Fit (10)
- Strong links to sibling and parent pages.
- Resource links support the conversion path.

7. Freshness and Governance (10)
- Clear last-updated/review ownership where appropriate.
- Outdated claims removed quickly.

### Content Scoring Bands

- `90-100`: elite authority page; publish-ready for top-competition SERPs.
- `75-89`: strong page; needs one focused depth/proof pass.
- `60-74`: acceptable baseline; requires major expansion.
- `<60`: thin/high-risk; rewrite required.

## UX Rubric (100 points)

1. Above-the-Fold Clarity (15)
- In 5 seconds user can answer: what you do, where, and what to do next.

2. Navigation and Wayfinding (10)
- Header and menus are low-cognitive-load.
- Users can move between pillar, subpillar, proof, and contact quickly.

3. Readability and Scannability (15)
- Section rhythm, heading quality, bullet usage, paragraph density.

4. Conversion Path Quality (15)
- CTA hierarchy and placement are consistent.
- Form friction is minimized and disclosures are clear.

5. Mobile UX Quality (15)
- Tap/scroll behavior, menu reliability, form ergonomics.

6. Accessibility Baseline (10)
- Keyboard reachability, semantic headings, contrast, focus states.

7. Performance and Perceived Speed (10)
- Stable loading behavior and no obvious interaction jank.

8. Trust and Risk Reduction UX (10)
- Legal disclaimers, privacy confidence, proof modules, expectation setting.

### UX Scoring Bands

- `90-100`: elite conversion UX with strong trust architecture.
- `75-89`: strong; needs targeted IA or friction reduction.
- `60-74`: works but crowded or inconsistent.
- `<60`: redesign required.

## Minimum Standards by Page Type

1. Pillar pages (`/criminal-defense`, `/personal-injury`)
- Target depth: 2,500+ words of unique on-page content.
- Must include: proof block, process block, linked subpillar map, FAQs, top+bottom CTA.

2. Service subpillars (`/criminal-defense/*`, `/personal-injury/*`)
- Target depth: 1,500+ words.
- Must include: strategy sections, process steps, FAQs, related links, risk framing.

3. Market service subpillars (`/market/criminal-defense`, `/market/personal-injury`)
- Target depth: 1,200+ words with at least 35% market-specific language.
- Must include unique local context: venue/county behavior, corridor/incident patterns, local risk conditions.

4. Resource guides (`/resources/*`)
- Target depth: 1,800+ words.
- Must include practical checklists, timeline decisions, and direct links into money pages.

## Weekly Scoring Cadence

- Score top 20 commercial URLs every week.
- Score any newly published page before release.
- Record deltas against prior week to verify improvement velocity.
